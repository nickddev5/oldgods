## Canon this game must keep

From *The Empty Throne*'s bible, "Before the throne: the old gods" (decided by Nick, 2026-10-09):

- Elias was a god among many old gods. The throne stood empty the whole time; the old gods never shared it.
- Elias is the throne's first holder. Who made the throne is not revealed.
- Elias sits because he believes it is the right decision.
- When he sits, the old gods' power combines with his and they disappear. They live on inside him.
- The Last Test is a construct that protects the throne from the unworthy.

## Premise (menu, first launch, and the Lore page)

> Before anyone held the throne, there were the old gods. Many of them, each with a domain and a people.
>
> Between them stood a throne no one had claimed. It stood empty through every age, guarded by a construct they called The Last Test.
>
> Now the old gods walk the road to it. Some go to see it. Some go to break its guardian. One will sit.
